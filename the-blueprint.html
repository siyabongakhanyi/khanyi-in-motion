THE PERSONAL SERIES / 01 — 04

FOUR STATES.
ONE COLOUR.

Not just the colour I wear. The colour that lets me feel more than one thing at once.

STATE 01 / THE SOFT BLUE

STILLNESS.

“Some days I reach for blue because I need a little room to breathe.”

A quieter kind of confidence. Nothing to prove, nowhere to rush.

STATE 02 / THE FADED BLUE

MEMORY.

“Other days, blue feels like a memory I can't quite name.”

Old pieces, new stories. Some things stay with me even when everything changes.

STATE 03 / THE ELECTRIC BLUE

DEFIANCE.

“I can be soft and still take up space.”

Blue can make a statement without asking to be heard.

STATE 04 / THE MOVING BLUE

RELEASE.

“And sometimes, I just want to move. No reason. No permission.”

The mood shifts. The layers move. The colour comes with me.

MY STYLE.
