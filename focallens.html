THE STORY / WITHOUT A BRIEF

THE WORK
HAPPENED
IN REAL TIME.

There was no shot list, no elaborate set and no planned campaign. She did her makeup. I picked up the Canon EOS R50. We followed the light, her expressions and the ideas arriving between photographs.

For Khanyi FocalLens Media, the instinct was to notice and direct moments as they appeared. For Elegance in Blends, the work was transforming colour and expression across two makeup looks, while also being the subject in front of the camera. These photographs were made through camera settings rather than a post-production retouching process.

THE ARTIST AND THE SUBJECT

ELEGANCE
IN BLENDS.

Two makeup looks in a single day. Fast colour decisions, confidence in the process and a face full of expression.

She created and wore both looks. The first used purple eye makeup, followed by olive tones coordinated with her top. Her ability to change the work quickly and move naturally in front of the camera shaped the photographs as much as the lighting did.

We picked up the makeup brushes already within reach. I asked her to hold them, move them and play with how they framed her face. Nothing had to feel scripted.
