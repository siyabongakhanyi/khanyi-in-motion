COMFORT
HAS MILEAGE.

The interesting part wasn't simply finishing the run. It was what I noticed along the way.

Crocs were already part of my movement, gym and everyday life. I went on to complete my second official 10K in a Classic Lined Overpuff Clog. That experience gave me a question worth exploring: could the comfort people recognise in Crocs inform something intentionally designed and tested for movement over distance?

01 / LIVE ITAuthentic use.

Personal experience before a collaboration brief.

02 / DOCUMENT ITShow the work.

Real photographs, motion and specific observations.

03 / QUESTION ITDesign the test.

Turn comfort into questions worth validating.

My observation: foot room, perceived pressure, heel security and comfort over time mattered. Two races under different conditions cannot prove a performance benefit. That is why the next step is structured testing, not a marketing claim.
