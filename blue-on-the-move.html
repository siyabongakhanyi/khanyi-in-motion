CASE STUDY / AN INDEPENDENT CROCS IDEA

COMFORT
HAS MILEAGE.

A product experience became a question worth putting in front of a brand.

Crocs were already part of my gym and everyday movement. I completed my second official 10K in a Classic Lined Overpuff Clog. The experience made me curious about foot room, perceived pressure, heel security and comfort over distance — and how those qualities could be investigated through an intentionally designed movement test.

THE OPPORTUNITY / FROM USE TO EXPERIMENT What if the comfort people already associate with Crocs could inspire a movement product worth testing?

The proposal: a bounded creator-led pilot, real-world observation, documented experience and honest qualitative feedback — not a demand for sponsorship or an unsupported performance claim.

The Crocs 10K observation and the HOKA Half photographs above are distinct experiences. Different races and conditions cannot establish a performance advantage.
